Questions

When can I order?

Sunday through Thursday at 8 PM. Ordering closes automatically after that and reopens Sunday.

When will my rolls arrive?

Saturday between 7 and 11 AM. We can't promise an exact time within that window.

Where do you deliver?

Anywhere within 20 miles of West Fargo, ND.

How do I pay?

With Venmo to @ellinelson1 when you place your order. Your order is confirmed once the payment comes through.

What if I'm not home?

No problem! Your cinnamon rolls will be left on your front step. Add delivery notes when you order if there's a better spot.

How do I keep and reheat them?

They stay fresh for up to 2 days after delivery. Keep them in the fridge since the icing has cream cheese. Warm one in the microwave for 15–30 seconds.
